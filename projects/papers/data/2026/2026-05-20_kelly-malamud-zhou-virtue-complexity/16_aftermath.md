1. **"Less is more" 통념 의 *empirical reversal***. Fama, Cochrane 등 전통 finance 의 *parsimony* 원칙 → KMZ 의 *complexity virtue*. *Empirical evidence overrides 통념*. ML 의 *double descent* 가 finance 에 *적용 입증*.

2. **High-dim feature engineering 의 *common foundation***. Gu-Kelly-Xiu AE (94 characteristics, 64-128 hidden) = high-dim. Chen-Pelger-Zhu (FFN + LSTM) = high-dim. KMZ 의 *RFF random features* + *ridge* = high-dim. *Same paradigm* under different architectures.

3. **Foundation model = ultimate complexity**. TFM (Chronos) = millions of parameters. KMZ paradigm 의 *natural extension*. *Pre-training + fine-tuning* = high-dim + regularization. *KMZ theoretical justification* 이 *foundation era enabler*.
