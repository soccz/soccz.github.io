3. **Interpretable baseline + ensemble component**. Deep learning factors = black box. RP-PCA = *interpretable linear factors*. Production: *RP-PCA baseline + deep learning enhancement* 의 *ensemble*. *Robustness check* + *interpretability anchor*. 2026 에서도 *practical relevance*.
